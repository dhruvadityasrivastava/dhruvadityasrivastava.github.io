## What I work on

### Biologicals & Nutrition
I study how soil microbes and inoculants trade nutrients with crops and help them cope with stress. At Penn State I look at how plant nutrition shapes the support maize gets from arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi. At Missouri I ran ¹³C-labelling studies of carbon and nutrient exchange in mycorrhizal networks and led three RNA-seq projects on drought stress. Before that, in Israel, I worked on the grey-mould fungus *Botrytis cinerea* and on the biocontrol yeast *Pseudozyma aphidis*.

### Data Science & Ag R&D
I turn plant data into decisions: randomised greenhouse and field trials, analysed in R and Python with RNA-seq and WGCNA co-expression networks, ionomics, microbiome sequencing with QIIME, and machine learning. I completed the UT Austin postgraduate program in AI and machine learning and build my own tools, from SVM feature-selection pipelines to a neural network that identifies plant seedlings from photos. See [Projects](./projects.md).
