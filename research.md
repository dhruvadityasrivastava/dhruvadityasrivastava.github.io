## Research areas
*   **Mycorrhizal Symbiosis** - How plant nutrition shapes the role of arbuscular mycorrhizal (AM) fungi in abiotic stress tolerance.
*   **Fungal Pathogenesis** - Genes *Botrytis cinerea* needs to infect crops (Nature Communications, 2019).
*   **AI & Machine Learning** - ML pipelines and AI tools for plant data, and deep learning in seismology.
*   **Biocontrol & Microbiome** - How beneficial microbes like *Pseudozyma aphidis* protect plants.
